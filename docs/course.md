# AiTutor Course Structure

This document outlines the current curriculum structure of the AiTutor platform. It details the 30 available phonetic modules and their respective chapters based on the active course data.

## Module 1: Phonetic Sound: /æ/

**Subtitle:** Learn the sound and connect it to words!
**Accent Color:** `#58CC02`

### Chapters

_Note: Chapters marked as "Text Lesson" can function differently. If a text lesson does not contain audio phonemes or an audio url (i.e. informational only), the flow will automatically advance when the speech finishes, and the user will not see Replay or Continue buttons. Text lessons with audio (often named "Listen") will display these controls._

1. **Introduction to Phonetics**
   - **Type:** Text Lesson (Auto-advancing)
   - **Description:** Friendly introduction to phonetic sounds for young learners.
   - **Script:** "Hello my stars! Did you know there are 44 sounds in English? Today we begin our journey with the very first one — listen carefully — /æ/!"

2. **Learn /æ/ Words**
   - **Type:** Text Lesson
   - **Description:** Transition into learning words that use the /æ/ sound.
   - **Script:** "Brilliant! Now let's meet some words that have the /æ/ sound. Ready? Let's go!"

3. **Listen: Bat**
   - **Type:** Text Lesson
   - **Description:** Example word containing /æ/ (🏏).
   - **Script:** "Our first word is Bat! Listen closely."

4. **Say: Bat**
   - **Type:** Speaking Assessment
   - **Description:** Voice recording and pronunciation assessment for the word "Bat".
   - **Script:** "Now it's your turn! Say Bat!"

5. **Listen: Cap**
   - **Type:** Text Lesson
   - **Description:** Example word containing /æ/ (🧢).
   - **Script:** "Our next word is Cap! Listen closely."

6. **Say: Cap**
   - **Type:** Speaking Assessment
   - **Description:** Voice recording and pronunciation assessment for the word "Cap".
   - **Script:** "Amazing job! Now say Cap!"

7. **Listen: Pan**
   - **Type:** Text Lesson
   - **Description:** Example word containing /æ/ (🍳).
   - **Script:** "And our last word is Pan! Listen closely."

8. **Say: Pan**
   - **Type:** Speaking Assessment
   - **Description:** Voice recording and pronunciation assessment for the word "Pan".
   - **Script:** "You're doing so well! Say Pan!"

9. **Find /æ/ Words**
   - **Type:** Tap Word Exercise
   - **Description:** Identify words containing the /æ/ sound from a list.
   - **Script:** "Time to play detective! Can you find all the words with that sound? Tap them all!"

10. **Match Voice to Word**
    - **Type:** Match Pairs
    - **Description:** Match audio clips of words to their written form.
    - **Script:** "Listen carefully and match each voice to the right word. You've got this!"

11. **Match Picture to Word**
    - **Type:** Match Pairs
    - **Description:** Match emojis/pictures of words to their written form.
    - **Script:** "Now look at the pictures and find their matching words. Let's go!"
