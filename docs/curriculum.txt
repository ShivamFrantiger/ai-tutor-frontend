AiTutor Curriculum Overview
===========================

Welcome to the AiTutor Phonetics Course! This curriculum is designed to help young learners master English phonetic sounds through engaging listening, speaking, and interactive exercises.

Here is the structure of our current modules:


Module 1: Phonetic Sound /æ/
----------------------------
Objective: Learn the /æ/ sound and connect it to words!

Vocabulary Words: Bat, Cap, Pan

Chapters:
1. Introduction to Phonetics: A friendly introduction to the 44 English phonetic sounds, starting with /æ/.
2. Learn /æ/ Words: Transition into learning words that use the /æ/ sound.
3. Listen: Bat: Listen to the word "Bat".
4. Say: Bat: Students practice saying "Bat" and receive pronunciation feedback.
5. Listen: Cap: Listen to the word "Cap".
6. Say: Cap: Students practice saying "Cap" and receive pronunciation feedback.
7. Listen: Pan: Listen to the word "Pan".
8. Say: Pan: Students practice saying "Pan" and receive pronunciation feedback.
9. Find /æ/ Words: Identify words containing the /æ/ sound from a list.
10. Match Voice to Word: Match audio clips of words to their written form.
11. Match Picture to Word: Match pictures to their written form.
